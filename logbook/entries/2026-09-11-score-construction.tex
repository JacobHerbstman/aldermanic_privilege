\section*{Score construction and boundary ordering}
\textit{September 11, 2026. Agent-drafted robustness checks requested by Jacob.}

The paper's sequential score remains the main specification. This check
reproduces the through-2022 and frozen through-2014 scores and the original
main density, price and permit estimates before changing assignments.
Joint estimation uses the same complete permit observations, covariates and
fixed effects, with alderman effects included simultaneously. Both unshrunk
variants omit shrinkage, allowing its contribution to be separated from the
sequential versus joint adjustment choice.

Among 4,034 eligible construction projects, removing shrinkage changes the
ordering of four of 256 distinct unordered alderman pairs, affecting 129
projects. Joint estimation changes 85 pair orderings and affects 1,389 projects.
All-construction FAR changes from -0.097 ($p=0.034$) to -0.029 ($p=0.611$),
and DUPAC from -0.148 ($p=0.044$) to -0.012 ($p=0.876$), in log coefficients.
The joint specification changes the assignments of 75,907 of 240,713 rental
observations and 19,509 of 58,468 sale observations. The sale coefficient
remains positive and significant (0.037, $p=0.009$), while rent remains
insignificant. The pooled permit coefficient changes from -0.126
($p=0.010$) to -0.069 ($p=0.232$). These are substantive specification
sensitivities, not evidence that the preferred construction contains a coding bug.

The full own-project exclusion removes recorded matched permits before
recomputing permit-volume controls, the neighborhood adjustment, the second
stage and shrinkage. It required separate score fits for 2,855 matched projects.
Only two of 4,034 project assignments reverse: residential parcels
25171240210000 and 25171240250000, both comparing Howard Brookins with Carrie
Austin. All-construction coefficients become -0.09713 for FAR and -0.14829
for DUPAC; sample sizes and substantive conclusions are unchanged.
Multifamily coefficients are unchanged. Projects without permits in the recorded
score-source matches retain the baseline score; this check does not establish
that the matching file identifies every possible own-project permit.

Jacob approved reporting score-only uncertainty separately from combined
score and outcome uncertainty. The bootstrap has not yet been run. A proposed
shared calendar-block positive-weight bootstrap, with annual and two-year
blocks, awaits a decision on its temporal-dependence assumption. No bootstrap
confidence interval is claimed here. The manuscript and main score remain
unchanged.
