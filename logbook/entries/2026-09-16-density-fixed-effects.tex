\section*{Density under alternative geographic and time fixed effects}
\textit{September 16, 2026. Exploratory specifications requested by Jacob.}

Replacing calendar-year fixed effects with periods of joint service makes
the multifamily estimates more negative when local boundary-segment effects
are retained. With zoning controls, FAR moves from $-11.51\%$ ($p=0.069$)
to $-14.70\%$ ($p=0.040$), and DUPAC from $-11.68\%$ ($p=0.175$) to
$-17.40\%$ ($p=0.071$). This is not a reduction in sampling uncertainty:
the respective log-coefficient standard errors increase from 0.066 to
0.076 and from 0.091 to 0.105. Without zoning, the joint-service estimates
are $-10.39\%$ for FAR and $-14.36\%$ for DUPAC. Broader ward-pair effects
produce smaller multifamily estimates in every corresponding specification.
All-construction estimates remain negative across the grid, but precision
and magnitudes vary with both geographic and zoning controls.

A joint-service period is the uninterrupted overlap of the two serving
aldermen within one ward pair and ward-map era. Reelection of the same
aldermen does not split a period. Calendar-year effects adjust for common
year differences; joint-service effects instead allow each local political
pair-period its own intercept. These are different controls, not merely a
coarser version of the same time effects. Replacing local segments with
whole borders also changes the geographic comparisons. The models are not
selected for their significance.

Every specification uses the existing scores, ten 100-foot distance bins,
five demographic controls and ward-pair clustering. Estimates compare
0--100 feet on the more-stringent side with $-100$--0 feet; they are not
averages over the full 500-foot window. All models retain exactly 4,016
all-construction or 863 multifamily observations, with both density measures
usable. Zoning remains required for the common starting sample even when
its fixed effects are omitted. Parentheses below contain $p$-values.

Year uncertainty still matters when it changes a building's assigned
alderman, map, zoning or annual controls. Joint-service effects do not repair
count, area or construction-eligibility errors. The thirteen newly reviewed
influential records are unchanged in these estimates. No production model,
construction input, score or manuscript was modified.

confidence intervals and data report accompany the HTML and LaTeX exhibits.
Both baseline estimates and standard errors reproduce the current reference
results within $10^{-8}$. The README records the input hash and the Make
command holding the four upstream outputs fixed.
