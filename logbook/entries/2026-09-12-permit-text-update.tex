\section*{Permit numbers in the manuscript}
\textit{September 12, 2026. Agent-drafted record.}

The paper's manually entered permit numbers lagged behind its generated
figures and tables. With Jacob's approval, the text now reports the main
coefficient as $-0.127$, equivalent to an 11.9\% decline. The separately
estimated changes are a 20.7\% decline under more stringent aldermen and a
7.0\% increase under more lenient aldermen. Including wards with alderman
turnover gives an 8.3\% decline.

The pretrend test $p$-values are 0.400 for the main sample, 0.075 for
low-discretion permits, and 0.666 when including alderman turnover. The
balance-test $p$-value is 0.333. These corrections synchronize the prose and
captions with the existing estimates; signs and significance conclusions are
unchanged. The data, models, and plotted figures are unchanged.
