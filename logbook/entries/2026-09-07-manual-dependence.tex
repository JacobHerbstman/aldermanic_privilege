\section*{September 7, 2026: Reducing manual construction decisions}
\textit{Agent-drafted audit; general-rule candidates have not been adopted.}

The restored construction inputs contain 48 decision or review CSVs with
1,088 rows, excluding three preserved source or derived snapshots. This is a
count of recorded entries, not distinct projects or unavoidable human judgments.
Projects recur across stages, and some entries repeat source measurements or
confirm earlier calculations. Jacob requested general rules wherever possible,
with case-specific ledgers as a last resort.

Two comparisons identify concrete opportunities. Among 273 external-review
records, 222 agree with the preceding classification and add no different unit
or building-area value. The other 51 have a classification difference, a value
change, or an unresolved disposition. There are 23 classification differences
and 32 rows with numerical changes; these sets overlap. The preceding input
already incorporates earlier adjudications, so agreement does not establish
independence from all manual decisions. Nor does it validate completion timing
or the land denominator.

Among 42 manual episode decisions, 39 retain the computed card inventory. All
39 match the already-computed disposition, unit count and building area.
Thirty-eight also request suppression of matched successors; one does not.
The remaining three depend on separate value overrides. A general rule may
replace the repeated recipe only after checking membership, matching ambiguity
and suppression on the expanded two-year groups. The two comparisons overlap
and cannot be added into a count of resolved projects.

Other candidates include extracting fields from consistently selected Assessor
vintages, applying study-period restrictions to reconstructed construction
histories, and recalculating zoning from ordinance dates after a year correction.
The main commercial ledger has 61 retaining, splitting or merging entries;
59 cite an existing 2021 or 2024 commercial source for building area. That
suggests replacing copied measurements with source links, but does not by
itself validate the vintage or project-membership choice.

Some external notes expressly distinguish a whole development from the smaller
parent phase. The intermediate review nevertheless substitutes development-wide
counts, such as 70 for a 43-card parent and 47 for a 39-card parent. The inspected
frozen final file contains 43 and 39. This requires a consistent rule about what
each source measures and when corrections apply, rather than accepting every
nonmissing external value. No production decision changed in this audit.

The number of unavoidable new human judgments remains unestablished. Neither
51 incremental differences nor 1,088 recorded entries measures that number.
The next comparison should test general rules against all relevant records and
leave only the residual evidence conflicts for human review.
