\section*{September 7, 2026: Assemble residential Assessor candidates}
\textit{Agent-drafted entry; existing research choices are preserved.}

The residential Assessor branch now builds a common source history and a table
that puts project membership beside its initial measurements. The source-preparation
task retains all 12,320,011 reports in the currently pinned full CSV, including
assessment year, reported construction year, parcel and card identifiers, units,
and areas. Five readers now use that typed history. Two readers' repeated
2022/2025/later selections have become a single ranking operation, preserving the
existing priority. The earlier-report discovery rule remains separate.

One producer now assembles the ordinary, tied-parcel, and multiple-card project
forms before the existing permit reconciliation. The actual Make build produced
27,831 candidates with component parcels, source records, construction year, units,
building area, land area, and measurement provenance. This count includes records
outside the study period. Existing rules mark 12,904 mechanically retained, 14,728
outside the period, 190 requiring further evidence, and 9 deferred to commercial
reconciliation. These are intermediate labels: mechanically retained records can
still be duplicates, and unresolved records may admit general rules rather than
manual decisions. Another 255 class-297 candidates and 45 residential/commercial
overlaps enter the existing downstream resolver.

A controlled comparison held the pinned source, geographic inputs, and permit
inputs fixed. Fourteen of the fifteen existing CSV products were byte-identical.
The 6,520-card table changed only row order; every keyed record and the downstream
candidate measurements agreed. The actual production candidate build also agreed
exactly with the replay. Two fresh source-preparation builds also produced identical Parquet bytes and
reports after fixing the writer's row-group layout. Standard reports check the
saved datasets and their keys.
Deleting three companion outputs caused their producers to regenerate them in a
parallel GNU Make 3.81 build. Unchanged builds ran no substantive producer.

This establishes a working connection between source history, membership, and
initial measurements for these residential project forms. It does not finish the
new-construction dataset. Completion timing, predecessor/successor duplication,
and measurement scope still require reconciliation. The distance carried here is
preliminary geographic evidence; final density and ward-boundary assignment must
follow the resolved project facts. No new case-specific decisions or exclusions
were introduced, and the paper's frozen input and estimates were not replaced.
