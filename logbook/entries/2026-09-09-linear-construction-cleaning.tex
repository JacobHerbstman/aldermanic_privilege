Jacob chose a chronological construction workflow: read sources and remove empty
cards; identify distinct buildings; select complete assessment measurements;
apply remaining recorded corrections; calculate density; and attach
construction-year ward-boundary distances. Identity-related year decisions stay
before duplicate matching, because they help establish which records describe
the same construction episode. Other measurement corrections follow assessment
selection. Final geography cannot revise physical measurements.

The active decision inputs have been reduced from 30 CSVs containing 685 rows to
26 containing 666 rows. These counts include source links, exclusions, retained
source choices and locations; they are not numbers of distinct manually corrected
buildings. Three overlapping residential measurement inputs now feed one table
with 53 source-to-building rows for 49 final buildings. Two commercial measurement
inputs are combined, omitting five superseded entries. Eight additional
whole-project decisions already superseded by component decisions are preserved
outside the active input. The commercial producer rejects competing correction
coverage, and residential final corrections cannot overwrite measurements already
reviewed during identity construction.

The early commercial discovery file now preserves source measurements. Its two
unit overrides and its low-density land heuristic were removed from that stage;
final commercial cleaning owns the reported unit and land choices. This restores
raw unit values on two preliminary rows and raw land values on three, without
changing final building measurements. Forty-seven obsolete map-derived numeric
land values were also cleared from active decision inputs. Approved reported-land
selection and external reported measurements remain; maps establish location and
identity, not density denominators.

The rebuilt combined data contain the same 13,764 project records as immediately
before this cleanup. Every existing project-ledger field agrees, including units,

finished measurement file: floor area divided by land area, and homes times 43,560
divided by land area. No additional building review or measurement choice was made.
