\section*{September 7, 2026: Put analytical assignment after construction cleaning}
\textit{Agent-drafted entry; the remaining design recommendations are not new research decisions.}

The restored construction pipeline follows successive rounds of correction more
closely than the order of the underlying research questions. Three intermediate
scripts assigned aldermen, treatment scores, and ward controls before project
verification was complete. The final exporter discarded those assignments and
recomputed them. The intermediate assignments have been removed, along with
their source dependencies; the final exporter now owns them within construction
cleaning. Geographic evidence used for matching and review remains available.

A controlled ten-script replay held upstream project evidence and recorded
judgments fixed. Before and after this change, the final export contained the
same 8,648 projects and all 41 columns were byte-identical. The intermediate
project memberships, measurements, eligibility decisions, and classifications
also agreed. The replay used the original episode inventory and its recorded
judgments; it does not resolve the pending duplicate groups under the updated
matching rules or establish a complete rebuild from raw sources. The frozen
paper input was not replaced.

Inspection of import warnings also found that sparse review columns were guessed
as Boolean values. Explicit types restored 22 unit-count cells and 13 floor-area
cells in the classification evidence, and 248 classification-source labels in
the provisional sample. These are evidence and provenance fields; restoring them
left the final export unchanged. The affected readers now reject parsing failures.

A second replay simplified the construction-year rule at its source. The temporal
producer now summarizes numeric year evidence before export. The candidate producer
uses those summaries directly rather than parsing a display string twice, and it
constructs the parcel-membership table once instead of repeating the same operation
for five project types. All 27,947 candidates, 29,125 project/parcel memberships,
and 270 review-queue records were byte-identical. The temporal output adds three
summary fields, with its assessment snapshots unchanged.

The larger recommendation is to organize the remaining work around source history,
project identity, measurements, eligibility, construction-year geography, and
finally estimation. This is not yet the implemented architecture. In particular,
card totals are aggregated, replaced with an earlier selected card, and later
reconstructed; external development totals can describe a larger object than the
project being measured; and related permit applications can describe distinct
buildings. Consolidating those decisions requires examining their evidence and
scope before changing the rules. The construction README now gives that
chronological reading guide and identifies the remaining judgment calls.

producer rules in disposable fixtures and identical saved inputs on both sides.
Standard data reports were inspected. GNU Make 3.81 regenerated a deleted multicard
companion output in a parallel build; unchanged builds ran no producer. A dry run
with the score input marked changed scheduled only final assignment and its report.
Hashes and comparison scopes are recorded in the release audit's
\texttt{restored\_stage\_checks.csv}. The declared construction chain still has

vintage is not fully recovered. These checks establish the stated simplifications,
not release-ready replication or new paper estimates.
