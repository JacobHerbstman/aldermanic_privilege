\section*{September 9, 2026: Halsted and nine other construction records}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{September 9, 2026: Halsted and nine other construction records}
\textit{Agent-drafted review; recommendations below have not been applied.}

Halsted's original four apartments should be distinguished from a fifth dwelling created by converting commercial space. The 2016 permit revision specifies four original apartments, and a June 2017 permit records the later conversion. Original sales in February 2017 support that completion proxy. A May 2017 broker listing reports 22,532 square feet for the whole building, including commercial space; the completed Assessor records provide a shared lot of 5,483 square feet but no building total. The broker figure is a possible whole-building measure, not independently confirmed apartment floor area. Its brochure attachment was inaccessible.

The other nine reviews separate relatively clear identities from remaining measurement and timing questions. Kedzie's parent covers six already retained individual homes, corroborated by two three-home permits. Liberty's parent covers five already retained townhomes, matching its five-home permit. Polk's parent covers three retained three-flats, but their 2008 years conflict with 2009 new-building permits. The old Harbor development parcel contains the successor parcel points used by Cascade and Cirrus; it does not establish another building. These findings support retiring old records once the stated identity links and any remaining timing questions are recorded.

Warren was built as two dwellings and converted to one house under a 2025 permit. Its earlier condo records supply two parcel identities, 2,586 square feet of building area and 2,827 square feet of land, rather than the later residential record's 3,025-square-foot measurement. Lake Park's exact parcel still has zero floor area; neighboring houses' measurements must not be copied. Honore combines conflicting old and new years with repeated six-apartment measurements, while the nearby new-building permit specifies four apartments. Its new-construction measurements remain unresolved.

Aqua's old nine-card record duplicates the nine retained Parkhomes, but their location in Aqua's podium means that the existing large land denominator needs a coverage decision; resolving the duplicate does not resolve density. Madison's current parcel records identify eighteen homes on twenty-seven parcels, but dates range from 2005 in the Assessor to 2006--2007 in listings. An individual sale in December 2006 does not alone settle completion. No pre-period exclusion was adopted.
