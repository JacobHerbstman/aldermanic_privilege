\section*{One construction correction input}
\textit{September 12, 2026. Agent-drafted record of the construction rewrite.}

Jacob rejected the thirteen-task reorganization because it preserved too much
of the review process in production. He requested one place to insert the
recorded replacements, followed by ordinary treatment of every building. The
previous version was committed and pushed at \texttt{bb91ea84} before the rewrite.

The thirteen intermediate tasks, containing 25 R scripts and 10,793 lines, have
been removed. The replacement task,
\texttt{prepare\_new\_construction}, has two scripts totaling 1,128 lines. The first reads the recorded
Assessor histories, removes empty cards, selects complete assessments, and
constructs the ordinary building records. The second reads the committed
correction CSV once, replaces the specified records and fields, resolves the
remaining ordinary identity matches, calculates FAR and DUPAC, and assigns
construction-year geography. The existing analysis-data task attaches aldermen,
controls, and construction-year zoning. No downstream task reads the correction
CSV.

The correction input contains 742 instructions, including updates, replacements,
and exclusions. This is not a count of additional buildings. Measurements from
explicitly reviewed assessment cards are recorded as replacement values with
their source-row references. The production build therefore does not repeat
those reviews. Unreviewed buildings continue to obtain their measurements from
the preserved Assessor sources. The source importer retains its separate,
documented correction to one mistyped parcel-reference number, applied once
before parcel relationships are constructed.

The previous 25 decision tables retain their original bytes in the construction
review archive. Consolidation distinguished active instructions from historical
notes that the checkpoint pipeline no longer applied. In particular, the older
additional-candidate table supplied only the cross-source replacement in that
pipeline; its other earlier proposals were not silently activated. The archive
records these distinctions and the one-time translation. Optional source searches
and the earlier review displays also remain in the audit archive.

The measurement comparison reproduces all 13,677 construction observations,
including their identifiers, component parcels, construction years, dwelling-unit
counts, floor areas, land areas, FAR, DUPAC, and density-measure eligibility.
Multifamily classification, location eligibility, wards, ward pairs, boundary
eras, and membership in the 500- and 1,500-foot windows also agree. The largest
boundary-distance difference is 0.0000031 feet from numerical rounding; the
corresponding boundary-characteristic differences are below $2\times10^{-9}$.

The analysis dataset retains 9,493 rows and the same regression variables. The
main regression sample remains 4,023 observations, including 863 multifamily
observations. One source-class label, for parcel 17073250490000, now reflects the
default Assessor snapshot (OA2) rather than the reviewed card's class (211). Its
recorded multifamily classification is unchanged. Table 1's CSV and LaTeX are
identical, and the main density figure is identical when rendered. The placebo
and donut figures reproduce their displayed estimates; the boundary-check and
score-robustness tables are identical.

The own-project permit check previously received parcel geometry under earlier
building IDs. It now reads the finished-building parcel file. Applying the same
parcel and date-window rule adds 28 links across 15 buildings, including 17 permit
IDs not previously linked anywhere; no old link is removed. Ten added links
belong to four buildings in the common 500-foot sample. Re-estimating the
project-specific score check leaves the published robustness table unchanged.

GNU Make 3.81 checks in a disposable copy cover a fresh task build, all nine
missing outputs individually, correction-only and raw-source propagation, an
unchanged rebuild, and parallel invocation. These checks caught an intermediate
source table being discarded; the task now lists its saved source tables
explicitly in \texttt{all}. Actual construction and analysis rebuilds succeed,
and subsequent builds execute no producers. All written GeoPackages include
the EPSG:3435 coordinate-system definition. The 25 archived decision tables
match their original bytes, and every consolidated instruction has a reason
and a source reference.

The paper Makefile rebuilds all four PDFs in the repository's paper directory.
Their extracted text matches the checkpoint, and all 56 rendered pages of the
working paper are pixel-identical. The manuscript source was unchanged.
