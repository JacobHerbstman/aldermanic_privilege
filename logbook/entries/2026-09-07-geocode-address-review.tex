\section*{September 7, 2026: Review street matches and restore parcel lookup}
\textit{Agent-drafted audit; historical-coordinate priority remains a recommendation.}

The geocoder review confirms four street-label mismatches, but does not establish
that all four old locations were wrong. Both acceptance scripts now compare house
number, direction, street name, and street type after abbreviation normalization,
allowing an omitted trailing unit label. This flags four Census responses and
reduces accepted Census requests from 194 to 190. All 114 Chicago acceptances and
their coordinates are unchanged. Three requests lose their reference points;
the fourth still has an accepted City point. These are unresolved locations at an
intermediate stage, not new density exclusions.

The pinned permit history supports the alternative street labels. At 1236 S Troy
Street, a July 2007 permit describes a new three-unit building. At 3609 W 50th
Place, a November 2007 permit describes four townhomes; a March 2008 amendment
changes its description to one unit. Another March 2008 amendment changes an
address from 3619 to 3623 W 50th Place. A 2025 roofing permit identifies 763 W 15th
Street and a successor parcel. No permits in the pinned source match the four
original full street labels. These records corroborate addresses, but do not by
themselves prove links between historical parent parcels and successors. The
previous entry's description of the City's 3623 match as the ``correct requested
street'' should be read only as agreement with the query, not independent proof
of the physical address. No units or construction years were changed in this review.

The preferred predecessor lookup also required one additional spatial query. It
returns a unique 2007 polygon, parcel 17073250410000. The combined source preserves
all 1,693 original features and adds this one polygon. Relative to the archived
selected polygons, all 3,768 shared shapes are spatially equal; two selections
disappear after rejecting the mismatched addresses and the new query adds one.
Two shared shapes have different binary serializations despite spatial equality.
The lookup now builds from pinned source polygons and query scopes through Make.

An additional public-source extract contains 447 exact-parcel records from
1999--2025 for all 216 address requests. Choosing the nearest assessment year,
with the earlier year winning ties, supplies finite coordinates for 191 requests,
including three of the four street mismatches. The selected years are one to
eleven years from the requested year. Troy has no finite coordinate in this source
window. Codex recommends applying the existing nearest-year rule to these exact
parcel records before using address geocoding. Jacob's decision is pending: the
audit evaluates this priority, but production does not consume its selected points.
