% Developer choice and empirical predictions
\begin{frame}
    \frametitle{Predictions and Empirical Comparisons}
    \begin{enumerate}[itemsep=0.7em]
      \item \textbf{Permit activity:} fewer projects enter when expected regulatory delay increases.\\
        {\small Compare blocks reassigned in 2015 with nearby blocks that stayed.}
      \item \textbf{Completed density:} a given profitable project chooses a smaller scale.\\
        {\small Compare dwelling units per acre across ward boundaries.}
      \item \textbf{Housing costs:} less new supply can raise rents and prices.\\
        {\small Compare rents and sale prices across the same boundaries.}
    \end{enumerate}
    \vspace{0.5em}
    \small Processing time from \textbf{application to issuance} is a proxy for regulatory delay. Selection into construction and wider housing markets can affect the observed density and price comparisons.
\end{frame}
