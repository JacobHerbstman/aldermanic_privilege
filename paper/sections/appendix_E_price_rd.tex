\section{Additional Price Boundary Checks}
\label{sec:appendix_price_rd}

Figure~\ref{fig:price_placebo_boundaries} moves the cutoff 1000ft inside each side of the true ward boundary.
The placebo windows do not overlap the main 500ft sample.
None of the nearest-bin placebo estimates is statistically distinguishable from zero.

\begin{figure}[H]
    \centering
    \includegraphics[width=0.98\textwidth]{../tasks/price_boundary_results/output/price_boundary_placebos.pdf}
    \caption{Placebo Cutoffs for Listed Rents and Home Sale Prices}
    \figurenotes{The left column places an artificial cutoff 1000ft inside the less-stringent ward; the right column places it 1000ft inside the more-stringent ward.
    The top row reports listed rents and the bottom row reports home sale prices.
    Each model uses a 500ft window around the artificial cutoff and otherwise follows the corresponding specification in Figure~\ref{fig:price_boundary_bins}.
    Each point is the estimated difference between a 100ft distance band and the band immediately below the artificial cutoff.
    Shaded areas are 95\% confidence intervals.
    Standard errors are clustered by boundary segment.}
    \label{fig:price_placebo_boundaries}
\end{figure}

Figure~\ref{fig:price_straight_boundaries} restricts the sample to locations where the ward boundary is approximately straight.
The estimates are somewhat larger than the full-sample results.

\begin{figure}[H]
    \centering
    \includegraphics[width=0.98\textwidth]{../tasks/price_boundary_results/output/price_boundary_straight.pdf}
    \caption{Price Comparisons Along Straight Ward Boundaries}
    \figurenotes{A boundary is classified as locally straight when both endpoints of a 100-meter line tangent to the nearest boundary point lie within 15 meters of the actual boundary.
    The models otherwise follow Figure~\ref{fig:price_boundary_bins}.
    Shaded areas are 95\% confidence intervals, and standard errors are clustered by boundary segment.}
    \label{fig:price_straight_boundaries}
\end{figure}

Figure~\ref{fig:price_boundary_donuts} excludes observations within 25ft or 50ft of the ward boundary.
The estimates are similar after either restriction.
