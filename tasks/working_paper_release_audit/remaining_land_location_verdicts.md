## How to read the review

“Clear this land/location flag” means the inspected parcel area and location evidence support closing that particular question. It does not independently certify the construction year, whole-building identity or every other release check. “Likely retain” means the building looks salvageable but the named evidence is still required. Prior review notes are explicitly identified as such; their fields are not treated as freshly verified merely because a ledger contains them. Several older notes conflict with the newer instruction to retain individually measured townhomes.

All current-point comparisons use the ward map appropriate to the candidate construction year, with the existing 2015 map convention. Later exact-PIN coordinates are not automatically accepted as historical building locations. Area agreement establishes parcel measurement, not necessarily the economic unit of observation.
