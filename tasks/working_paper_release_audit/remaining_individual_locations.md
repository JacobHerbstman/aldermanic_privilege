# The 69 individual-location exceptions

The production location rule resolves 62 of the 69 records identified in the September 8 comparison. It uses an exact parcel point from an assessment year with the selected building measurements, respecting committed year corrections, or an already verified exact Chicago address point. Seven locations remain unaccepted under those rules. This does not mean that seven buildings are fictitious or that all seven should be dropped.

The reproducible 69-row assessment comparison is `output/remaining_individual_location_findings.csv`; its standard report is in `report/`. It includes selected and latest measurements, available assessment/coordinate years, and the resulting location. The baseline IDs in `reference/individual_location_remaining_cases.csv` preserve which 69 were investigated; they are not production exceptions. The following recommendations have not been applied as exclusions or replacement measurements.

Other later floor-area differences in the 69 are documented in the CSV. For those with matching earlier assessment-year points, the rule locates the property from that earlier assessment and preserves the selected measurement. A later change does not by itself establish whether the earlier value was erroneous, a different measurement convention was used, or an alteration occurred. No new unit counts, land allocations or floor-area replacements were invented.

Eight Campbell homes already have approved 2007 years in the committed construction-year ledger. Those are not new year disputes. South Troy's direction correction is also already recorded and respected by the Chicago address producer.
