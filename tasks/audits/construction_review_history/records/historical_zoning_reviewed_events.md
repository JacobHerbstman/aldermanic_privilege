# Recorded historical zoning judgments

These three CSVs transcribe existing decisions from `build_project_construction_zoning.py`
at research-archive commit `010a1f8497c1f32e2c79b5933d1c5baf9af44be3`.
They do not record a new review or new approval by Jacob. The event ledger retains
all 14 original PIN/ordinance decisions, dates, source PDFs and review notes.
Its replacement flag identifies the three parser links superseded by those reviews.
The five exact-preconstruction support IDs and one recovered ordinance date
were also embedded in the original script and are now explicit inputs.

The original five-ID support list did not include separate row-level review notes.
The recorded reason identifies that limitation; it is not invented source evidence.
Further substantive changes require evidence and a documented decision.
The restored producer validates these inputs and checks event dates against the
recorded ELMS matters file. End-to-end ordinance extraction remains unfinished.
