# Sale prices and rents with uncertain score orderings

This audit extends the density and permit checks to the paper's sale-price and rental boundary regressions. Run `make` in `code/`; the report is `output/price_score_sensitivity.html`. One estimation script runs separately for each market; one script displays the results. Production outcomes, scores, and manuscript are unchanged.

## Sources and specifications

The two source panels are the recorded copies in `data_raw/score_robustness/`. Their original producers and hashes are recorded in `../score_pair_reliability/sources/benchmark_sources.csv`. The baseline scores, complete score draws through 2022, and pair stability measures are concrete Make dependencies on that upstream audit. This consumer does not re-estimate the scores. The upstream audit documents the property, citywide quarter and citywide year reweightings and refitting both stages and shrinkage. Annual reweighting uses 17 years.

Sales cover 2006–2022, with the existing price, location, score, property-class and complete-control requirements. Rents cover 2014–2022, retain the existing clean-location flag and assignment-date restriction, and include nonnegative recorded bedrooms, positive floor area and bathrooms, and the existing building-type classification. Outcomes are positive prices; neither uses a zero-handling transformation. Both use ten 100-foot bins inside a strict 500-foot window. The reported coefficient compares [0,100) with [−100,0). Sales use segment-by-quarter fixed effects and rents segment-by-month fixed effects, with the existing property characteristics, amenities, property-type indicators and ward-pair clustering. All substantive specification arguments are in Make.

Rental observations can repeat for the same home over time; counts are not numbers of distinct homes. The audit defines named alderman pairs using the incumbents recorded for each observation. Different incumbents along the same geographic boundary remain separate comparisons. Counts describe sample representation, not regression leverage.

## Experiments and checks

1. **Complete score draws:** Apply all 499 draws per method to each market. Each draw supplies one coherent citywide ranking. Only the signs of distances change. Prices, controls, locations and the baseline fitted observations remain fixed. Missing or tied score differences stop the run, and every reversal must retain the baseline fitted rows. The middle 95% of draws describes score-only variation, not combined score-and-outcome uncertainty. Positive-draw shares are descriptive, not probabilities that a causal effect is positive.
2. **Fragile groups:** Reuse retention below 75%, and separately below 95%, for all three score methods. These choices precede inspection of price outcomes. Drop every observation belonging to the selected named pairs, or retain all observations and reverse their signs. Dropping changes the population; any additional singleton removal follows the original fixed-effect estimator and is visible in fitted versus input counts. Artificially reversing selected pairs may be inconsistent with a citywide ranking; it is a stress test, not an adopted score.
3. **Every pair individually:** Drop and reverse every pair within each market, including stable pairs. The report highlights the ten largest absolute changes per market but retains every result. These nonlinear percentage changes are not additive contributions to an estimate.

The baseline score signs must reproduce the recorded direction of every observation. Reversing all boundaries must negate the coefficient and preserve the standard error to numerical tolerance. The original estimator determines baseline fitted rows; later score-only fits must use exactly those rows. Keys, score-match coverage and missingness are checked. The recorded current benchmark is the `paper_full` version in the earlier developer comparison, which reproduces the production price specification.

Each market's `*_pairs.csv` reports counts and three retention shares. Its `*_pair_sensitivity.csv` records the original estimate, all grouped and individual tests, and conditional clustered uncertainty. Its `*_score_draws.csv` records every refit under the complete score vectors. The renderer creates the combined draw summary, combined pair-influence table, figure and HTML report. SaveData creates reports when saving durable tables. No logs or reports are Make targets.

All conditional p-values and intervals treat the tested sample and ordering as fixed. These checks preserve the chosen sequential score estimator and do not settle its interpretation or the sensitivity to joint estimation of neighborhood controls and alderman effects. Outcome sampling uncertainty has not been added to the score-draw ranges.
