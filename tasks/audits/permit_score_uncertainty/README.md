# Permit event study with uncertain score orderings

This audit carries the saved pre-remap score vectors through the paper's main high-discretion permit event study and pooled signed specification. It also drops and reverses uncertain pairs together and every informative pair separately. Run `make` in `code/`; the report is `output/permit_score_sensitivity.html`. Two scripts estimate and display the results. Production scores, outcomes, and manuscript are unchanged.

## Sources and sample

The recorded block panel is `data_raw/score_robustness/permit_block_year_panel_2015.parquet`; its original producer and hash are recorded in `../score_pair_reliability/sources/benchmark_sources.csv`. The audit reads that task's baseline scores through 2014, saved score draws, and pair-retention results through concrete Make prerequisites. Scores are not re-estimated by this consumer. The existing upstream audit documents the positive property, citywide quarter and citywide year weights and refitting of both stages and shrinkage. The annual score exercise has only nine time blocks.

The baseline keeps stable incumbents, distance at most 500 feet, event years −5 through +5 around 2015, and positive total high-discretion applications before the remap. Outcomes include zeros; Poisson is used without a log transformation. Both specifications include block and geographic boundary-pair-by-year fixed effects and cluster by geographic boundary pair. The event study omits 2014; the pooled signed coefficient covers 2015–2020 and imposes equal-and-opposite log effects across reassignment directions. These are the existing paper choices.

The original filtered panel contains 3,379 blocks, including 687 reassigned blocks. Poisson omits 339 block-years in all-zero fixed-effect groups, leaving 36,830 fitted observations across 95 geographic pairs. There are 44 pairs with reassigned blocks. The audit keeps the original fitted observations in every score-only reversal, checks that pooled and annual models use identical observations, and verifies that their all-boundary reversals negate coefficients and preserve standard errors. The recorded source scores must reproduce every original treatment direction.

## Experiments

- **Full score draws:** Carry all 499 draws for each of property, quarter and annual reweighting through both specifications. Each draw uses one complete citywide score vector. Reassigned blocks change direction as warranted; unchanged blocks remain at zero. Outcomes, observed locations, years, and fixed effects stay fixed. Every fit must converge and retain the baseline sample and all expected coefficients. The middle 95% of draws describes score-only variation, not combined score-and-outcome uncertainty. Annual curve ranges are pointwise, not simultaneous bands. Draw frequencies are descriptive, not posterior probabilities.
- **Fragile groups:** Apply the same retention-below-75% and below-95% thresholds as the multifamily audit, separately for all three methods, before examining permit outcomes. Dropping a pair removes all its reassigned and unchanged comparison blocks. Reversing a pair keeps all its blocks and changes only the sign for reassigned blocks. No uncertain pair is reclassified as untreated. Artificially reversing selected pairs may conflict with a single citywide ranking; it is a stress test, not an adopted score. Dropping changes the represented population.
- **One pair at a time:** Drop and reverse each of the 44 informative pairs, including stable ones. The complete annual path, pooled coefficient and joint pretrend test are retained. The display ranks pairs by their largest absolute pooled-coefficient change only as an influence diagnostic; it does not select a preferred regression. These changes are not additive contributions to the nonlinear model.

## Outputs and interpretation

`permit_score_draws.csv` records every draw's pooled and annual estimates. `permit_pair_sensitivity.csv` records the baseline, grouped tests and individual tests, with conditional clustered uncertainty, fitted counts and pretrend tests. `permit_pairs.csv` gives both aldermen, ward pair, block counts, baseline ordering, and three retention shares. The renderer writes the draw summary, all one-pair pooled results, two figures and the HTML report. SaveData creates the durable data reports as side effects.

Conditional p-values and intervals treat each tested sample and ordering as fixed. The pretrend test jointly tests event years −5 through −2, using the same covariance and F-test convention as production. Score-only ranges omit outcome sampling uncertainty and do not establish that aldermen cause the differences in permit processing times. This audit preserves the chosen sequential score estimator and does not resolve its sensitivity to estimating neighborhood controls and alderman effects jointly.
