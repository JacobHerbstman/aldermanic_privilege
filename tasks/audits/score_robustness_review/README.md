# Raw processing times and a proposed score-robustness appendix

This exploratory audit compares unadjusted processing-time orderings with the paper's scores and assembles the completed outcome-sensitivity results for manuscript review. Run `make` in `code/`; open `output/score_robustness_review.html`. Two scripts compare raw times and assemble the review. No manuscript file is changed or adopted as a consumer.

## Raw comparison

The recorded permit source is `data_raw/score_robustness/permits_for_uncertainty_index.csv`; its producer and hash are in `../score_pair_reliability/sources/benchmark_sources.csv`. Existing baseline scores and fitted local pair counts are concrete prerequisites from that audit. The first script reproduces the exact through-2014 and through-2022 score samples: four observed months of alderman support before regression exclusions, the existing lagged workload construction, complete stage-one controls and fixed effects, and positive processing durations. Same-day permits contribute to workload counts but not to the processing-time regression or raw means. Every alderman's retained permit count and observed-month count must equal the saved score baseline. The matched samples contain 71,059 permits for 83 aldermen and 120,505 permits for 113 aldermen. No source or production sample is changed.

The primary raw measure is the arithmetic mean of application-to-issuance days, weighting each retained permit equally. Median days is a second measure; average log days is a third, allowing a comparison on the score's outcome scale without any regression adjustment. Geometric mean days is displayed as the exponentiated average log. No trimming or new outlier rule is introduced. Selecting the same complete-control sample makes the comparison about measurement rather than different records; the raw means do not condition on these controls.

Pairs are the named aldermen used by the actual construction, multifamily, sales, rent and permit-event-study samples. The event study uses through-2014 raw times and scores; the other outcomes use through 2022. Report both the fraction of pairs agreeing and the fraction of observations represented by agreeing pairs. Observations are projects, transactions, rental records or reassigned blocks as appropriate. These counts are not regression influence weights. A raw tie is neither agreement nor reversal and remains in the overall denominator. Every local pair has matched raw measurements; no pair is removed for disagreement or weak raw separation. All possible citywide pairs and citywide correlations are separate contextual comparisons, including aldermen who did not serve together.

Raw-day agreement is descriptive. The scores and raw averages use the same permits, so it is not independent validation of political behavior. Differences combine the log transformation, covariate adjustment, two-stage aggregation and empirical Bayes shrinkage. The mean-log comparison removes the first of those differences but does not isolate the contribution of each control. Period-specific scores have different support and should not be interpreted as a causal estimate of temporal stability.

## Manuscript review

The second script reads the complete, saved multifamily, permit, sales and rent sensitivity outputs through Make. It reports all three resampling methods, both established fragility thresholds, baseline estimates, score-draw ranges, deletion estimates and reversal stress tests. Its compact proposed table displays the annual method and 75% threshold for every outcome; the full results remain alongside it. This display choice does not select outcomes or pair sets by the estimated effect. It does not re-estimate the outcomes.

The HTML includes proposed wording for review, not applied manuscript changes. It recommends a short pointer from score construction to an appendix section on local ordering and its consequences for estimated effects. Raw-time comparisons are labeled descriptive. The earlier weak chronological split remains a separate question and is not replaced by a claim of temporal stability. The existing scores are held fixed as the estimation method; interpretation remains conditional on that choice.

Score-only ranges omit outcome sampling uncertainty and are not full confidence intervals. Perturbation frequencies are not probabilities that an ordering is true or simultaneous rank confidence sets. Conditional p-values treat each chosen sample and ordering as fixed. Deliberate pair reversals can conflict with a citywide ranking; dropping changes the sample. A negative or positive point pattern is distinguished from precision.

The first script writes alderman summaries, full pair comparisons, agreement counts, citywide correlations and a scatterplot. The second writes the combined robustness table and review HTML. SaveData writes durable reports when saving each table. The audit retains every pair and every specified measure, including ties and disagreements, and verifies agreement/reversal/tie totals.
