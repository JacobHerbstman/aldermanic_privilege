\section{Additional Density RD Checks}
\label{sec:appendix_density}

This appendix reports covariate balance, shifted-cutoff placebos, and donut checks for the density analysis.
All results use the final project ledger, the baseline stringency score, and the common FAR-DUPAC samples used in Table~\ref{tab:density_main_table}.

\subsection{Covariate Balance}

Table~\ref{tab:density_covariate_balance} compares observed neighborhood characteristics across the two sides of the boundary.
The differences are smaller in the multifamily sample, although its joint test narrowly rejects exact balance.
Balance is weaker for all construction, consistent with treating those estimates as supporting evidence rather than the paper's main density result.

\subsection{Shifted-Cutoff Placebos}

Figures~\ref{fig:rd_placebo_shift_neg} and~\ref{fig:rd_placebo_shift_pos} move the cutoff 1000ft into either side of the true ward boundary and estimate the same model in a 500ft window around the artificial cutoff.
The placebo estimates are imprecise and do not reproduce the multifamily discontinuities at the true boundary.

\subsection{Donut RD Checks}

Figures~\ref{fig:rd_donut_25ft} and~\ref{fig:rd_donut_50ft} exclude projects within 25ft or 50ft of the ward boundary.
The multifamily estimates remain negative and statistically significant, so the main result is not driven only by projects immediately adjacent to the boundary.
